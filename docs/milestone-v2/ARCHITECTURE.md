# V2 architecture

The V2 source adds a distinct `create_covenant_v2` path to the existing Registry, Inspector and Judge. `create_covenant` retains the V1 single-source behavior. A fresh V2 deployment is required: the on-chain V1 contracts at the historical addresses are unchanged.

1. The Registry validates a policy with one canonical primary, one or two corroborators, and optionally a contextual source within a three-source total. Context never satisfies the corroboration threshold. It checks distinct sites and declared publishers, freezes canonical JSON and its SHA-256 digest, and escrows stake.
2. The Inspector independently renders each source, rejects empty or oversized rendered text, commits complete bounded rendered artifacts, and asks validators to reproduce source classifications and semantic findings. A compliant primary and the policy threshold are both required for baseline activation.
3. On challenge, the Inspector repeats the frozen source set and commits the incident artifacts. A definitive no-change result is provisional. A material change sends the same committed incident to the Judge; the Judge does not refetch a mutable page for V2 judgment.
4. The Judge independently re-evaluates primary breach semantics and corroboration over the committed incident. Validators compare outcome fields, classification and evidence digest. A supported breach or permitted change becomes provisional.
5. The authorized owner or challenger may post one equal bond within one hour. The Judge reconsiders the same incident artifacts with the bounded rebuttal. After a finalized appeal callback, or permissionless appeal-window expiry, the Registry computes credits and releases escrow. An appeal callback that misses its deadline fails closed as inconclusive.

The Registry authenticates Inspector/Judge callback senders and matches request IDs, stages, policy digest and evidence digest. V2 economic rules are frozen at covenant creation. Read methods expose policy, artifacts, provisional states, bond and credit accounting. Frontend V2 controls activate only when the deployment manifest identifies a V2 deployment.

Source independence is conservatively screened by distinct site suffixes and declared publisher names. This is **not a proof of beneficial ownership or editorial independence**. The rendered-text digest proves what validators committed, not what a remote publisher cryptographically signed. These limitations remain release concerns. Corrected source-matched V2 contracts and component bindings are finalized on Studionet, and the production frontend is live. Finalized V2 baseline, challenge, appeal and settlement receipts are recorded in `LIVE_VERIFICATION.md`; a controlled source change and breach payout also finalized via the retained single-source compatibility path.
